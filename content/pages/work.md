## Research

### Glucose curves and cognitive function

Laber Labs, Duke · 2023 · [Read the report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Xtd3oHxK-QWJOG8Jiyx2k4l7y_VrsKzI/view?usp=sharing)

Most studies reduce a day of glucose readings to a few averages. Working with a novel continuous-glucose dataset, I used functional regression to model cognitive function from the full curves instead, and recovered relationships the averages hide. **The work earned High Distinction in Statistical Science.**

### What lives in a primate's gut

Duke University · 2023 · [Read the report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1QOC08rLaYyZczz8IrrZNQW5ymS5TnfUv/view?usp=sharing)

Does a primate's gut microbiome vary by species, by season, or both? A linear mixed model, built to handle repeated samples from the same animals, found both. The work was submitted to the USRESP undergraduate research competition.

### US Gymnastics Olympic team selection

Duke University · 2023 · [Read the report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1EuGi5Gh2fxv_AMZMPvV-cgSgkZDdSsJa/view?usp=sharing)

Which five gymnasts give the U.S. its strongest medal prospects? Using international competition results, I developed a simulation framework to compare candidate rosters and explore the trade-off between individual medals and team success.
